# junglEngine privacy policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026.

junglEngine is made by Hawkstreak. This is the whole policy, and it is short
because the app does very little with data.

## The short version

- Your music never leaves your phone unless you export it and share it yourself.
- There are no accounts, no sign in, and no cloud sync.
- There are no ads, and no advertising or tracking identifiers are collected.
- Nothing you make, name, record or import is ever sent anywhere.

## What stays on your device

Everything you make. Your project, your patterns, your arrangement, and any
audio you import are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are
not uploaded, backed up by us, or readable by us.

Exported WAV and zip files are written to a temporary folder on your device and
handed to the system share sheet. Where they go from there is your choice, and
whatever you send them to has its own privacy policy.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## What is collected

Two things, both through Google Firebase, and both about the app rather than
about you.

**Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).** When the app crashes, a report is
sent containing the stack trace, the device model, the operating system
version, the app version, and a randomly generated installation identifier.
That identifier is not linked to you, to an account, or to any identifier used
for advertising. Crash reports never contain your projects, your audio, or any
file names.

**Anonymous usage counts (Firebase Analytics).** Four events, and no others:

| Event | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| `beat_created` | A Beat was made, whether it was Chop or Kit, and how many bars |
| `beat_opened` | A Beat was opened, and whether it was Chop or Kit |
| `scramble_tapped` | Scramble was tapped |
| `export_completed` | An export finished, and whether it was a loop, a song or the parts |

Alongside these, Firebase records the standard technical context it always
does: app version, device model, operating system version, and coarse country
from your IP address. Advertising consent is switched off in the app's code, so
no advertising identifier, no advertising storage and no personalisation
signals are collected.

None of these events carries a project name, a file name, a tempo, a pattern,
audio, or anything you typed.

## Purchases

junglEngine Pro is a one time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store or
Google Play. Hawkstreak never sees your payment details. The app stores a single
flag on your device recording that Pro was bought, so that it works offline.

## Files you import

Importing a break or a one shot copies that file into the app's own storage so
the app can keep playing it. It is not uploaded. If you remove it from the
project, the copy is deleted.

## Children

junglEngine is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data
from anyone.

## Your choices

- Turning off analytics and crash reporting: use your device's settings for
  limiting app tracking and diagnostics sharing.
- Deleting everything: delete the app.
